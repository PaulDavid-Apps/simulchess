Core Rules
1. Simultaneous submission
Each turn, both players secretly submit one move from the current board position.
2. Simultaneous reveal and resolution
Once both moves are submitted, they are revealed and resolved together as a single move-pair.
3. Standard movement patterns
Each submitted move must follow the normal movement pattern of the moving piece, based on the current board position.
4. King exposure is allowed
A move may expose its own king to capture.
5. Ignoring attack is allowed
A move may ignore an existing attack on its own king.
6. Moving into attack is allowed
A king may move onto a square attacked by an opposing piece.
7. No check or checkmate
There is no check, checkmate, stalemate-by-check, or forced response to an attack on the king.
8. Normal capture of a stationary piece
If a piece moves to capture an opposing piece, and that opposing piece does not move during the same move-pair, the opposing piece is captured.
9. Capture attempt against a moving piece
If a piece moves to capture an opposing piece, and that opposing piece also moves during the same move-pair, both moves happen and the opposing piece is not captured on its starting square.
10. Both pieces move to the same square
If both players’ pieces move to the same destination square, compare the normal material value of the pieces.
11. Higher-value piece wins destination conflict
The higher-value piece remains on the destination square. The lower-value piece is removed from the board.
12. Equal-value pieces in destination conflict
If both pieces have the same value, both pieces are removed from the board.
13. Suggested piece values for conflicts
Pawns are worth 1, knights and bishops 3, rooks 5, queens 9, and kings have special king-capture rules rather than a normal point value.
14. King capture ends the game
The game ends when a king is physically captured by a resolved move.
15. Single king capture
If exactly one king is captured during a move-pair, the player whose king was captured loses.
16. Double king capture
If both kings are captured during the same move-pair, the game is drawn.
17. Unresolvable non-terminal conflicts
If a move-pair creates an unresolvable conflict that does not involve king capture, both players must resubmit moves.
18. Repeated failed submissions
If players fail to produce a resolvable move-pair three times for the same turn, the game is drawn.
